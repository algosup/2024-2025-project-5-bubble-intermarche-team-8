This document aims to summarize all the takeaways from our project. It highlights areas where the team excelled, identifies weaknesses, and suggests improvements for future projects, from both personal and team perspectives.

## Key Achievements

- Delivered a functional, well-received product.
- Successfully balanced multiple roles.
- Gained first experience in QA documentation.
- Finished a complete project without burnout.
- Acquired solid foundational skills in Bubble and Figma.
- Delivered a strong, impactful presentation as a team.

## Challenges

- Communication breakdown across and within teams.
- Limited manpower due to small team size.
- Issues with motivation—both personal and team-wide.
- Difficulties in following up with app management.
- Lack of concrete task timelines.
- Personal demotivation.

## Root Causes

- Unclear instructions and excessive ambition led to confusion and misalignment.
- Team fragmentation and lack of shared responsibility.
- Low personal motivation at times due to external factors.
- Inexperience with structured planning and task delegation.
- General fatigue from the academic environment.

## Lessons Learnt

- The importance of clearly defined communication protocols.
- Gained practical skills in Bubble, Figma, QA, and time management.
- Learned to manage personal workload alongside collaborative expectations.
- Importance of early planning and task tracking.
- Recognized the need to take ownership and responsibility in a team context.
- Valued the impact of team vibe and emotional support on performance.

## Conclusion

This project allowed us to grow not just in technical capability, but also in soft skills such as time management, accountability, and communication. While the small team size posed challenges, it also gave us space to explore multiple roles and contribute meaningfully. Our final product and presentation reflect not just our work but our growth as a team. For future projects, clearer instructions, more structured planning, and sustained motivation will help us reach even greater heights.
